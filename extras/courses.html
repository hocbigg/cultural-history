Great Courses in Cultural History

This collection highlights standout university lecture series, OpenCourseWare curricula, and specialized digital courses in Cultural History. These audiovisual resources delve into the history of mentalities, everyday life, material and print culture, religious worldviews, and historical anthropology.

  • Mentalities, Beliefs, and Religious Worldviews
  • Everyday Life, Society, and Cultural Transformations
  • Material Culture, Media, and Visual Regimes
  • Cultural Theory, Memory, and Anthropological Perspectives

Mentalities, Beliefs, and Religious Worldviews

  • Introduction to the Old Testament / Hebrew Bible (Open Yale Courses / Christine Hayes)
  • Introduction to the New Testament History and Literature (Open Yale Courses / Dale B. Martin)
  • The Early Middle Ages, 284–1000 (Open Yale Courses / Paul Freedman)
  • Finding Britain's Lost Gods: Paganism, Folklore, and Ritual History (Gresham College / Ronald Hutton)
  • The Origins of Atheism: Doubt and Faith in the Renaissance and Reformation (Gresham College / Alec Ryrie)

Everyday Life, Society, and Cultural Transformations

  • France Since 1871 (Open Yale Courses / John Merriman)
  • Epidemics in Western Society Since 1600 (Open Yale Courses / Frank Snowden)
  • ChinaX: China's Past, Present and Future (edX / Harvard University / Peter Bol and William Kirby)
  • The Victorians: Culture and Experience in Britain, Europe and the World 1815–1914 (Gresham College / Sir Richard J. Evans)

Material Culture, Media, and Visual Regimes
